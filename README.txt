KG & Amount Calculator PWA

Files:
- index.html
- manifest.webmanifest
- sw.js
- icon-192.png
- icon-512.png

The app starts with 15 rows. Kilo and Total Amount calculate automatically.
Amount total subtracts the entered Amount deductions from Σ Total Amount.
Data is saved locally on the device.

To use as an iPhone Home Screen app:
1. Upload these files to an HTTPS static host.
2. Open the site in Safari.
3. Tap Share -> Add to Home Screen -> Add.
4. Launch it from the new Home Screen icon.

The app needs to be served over HTTPS for the service worker/PWA installation behavior.
